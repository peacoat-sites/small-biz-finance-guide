Sarah Johnson is a CPA who has worked exclusively with small businesses for over a decade, from sole proprietors to companies with 50 employees. She has seen the same tax mistakes made over and over by business owners who were never taught how business finances actually work, and her writing is aimed at closing that gap. At Small Biz Finance Guide, she covers accounting fundamentals, tax deductions, entity structures, and working with a CPA effectively.
